# Research & EDA Notebooks (`notebooks/`)

## Overview
Interactive Jupyter notebooks used for exploratory data analysis, prototype experimentation, and model evaluation.

## Suggested Organization
- `01_data_exploration.ipynb`: Exploratory analysis of resume datasets and vocabulary distributions.
- `02_skill_ner_experiments.ipynb`: Prototype training and evaluation of spaCy / HuggingFace NER pipelines.
- `03_semantic_similarity_benchmarks.ipynb`: Comparing sentence transformer embeddings on resume-JD pairs.
- `04_llm_prompt_prototyping.ipynb`: Developing and testing prompt templates for local LLMs.
